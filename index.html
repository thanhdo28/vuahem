FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Vuahem detect suspended or closed vendors? +

Vuahem continuously updates official tax administration gazettes and registry statuses from tax authority databases. The engine compares the vendor's legal operational status on the specific date the invoice was digitally issued.

Does Vuahem require direct write access to our accounting database? +

No. Vuahem is strictly non-intrusive. Data ingestion occurs via encrypted file drop (XML/PDF/CSV) or read-only API connectors. Your accounting records and ERP databases remain untouched.

Which non-cash payment thresholds are audited? +

The rule engine validates statutory non-cash payment rules (including standard 20M+ VND thresholds and updated statutory decrees), cross-matching wire transfer beneficiary account names against supplier tax entities.

What compliance standards and SLAs do you support? +

We adhere to Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and Vietnam Decree 13/2023 frameworks. Systems are hosted in Tier-III certified regional facilities with AES-256 encryption. Enterprise tiers include a 99.9% uptime SLA and dedicated support engineers.
